#**Decoding neurophysiological dynamics for parkinson’s disease Diagnosis via interpretable representation learning-based transformer**
  Neha Sharma, Kumar Gaurav and Tharun Kumar Reddy Bollu, Member, IEEE



The proposed work presents a multi-head self-attention-based deep learning framework for Parkinson’s disease classification and biomarker analysis. The study utilizes three EEG datasets acquired from the San Diego, Iowa, and UNM repositories. Initially, comprehensive preprocessing was performed on the datasets, including noise reduction and removal of power-line interference artifacts. Subsequently, the EEG signals were decomposed into five standard frequency bands: alpha, beta, gamma, delta, and theta.
For each frequency-specific dataset, a Transformer-based architecture incorporating multi-head self-attention mechanisms was implemented to capture discriminative spatiotemporal patterns associated with Parkinson’s disease. Furthermore, to enhance the interpretability of the proposed model, disease-related EEG biomarkers were visualized using topographic maps (topomaps) and polar plots. These visualizations provide comparative insights between healthy control subjects and Parkinson’s disease patients, facilitating a better understanding of the learned neural representations and underlying neurological alterations.
